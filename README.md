## About the Project

**Smart Telecom** is a front-end e-commerce website concept for a
business selling mobile phones and technology products. It brings key
storefront information together in a single-page layout, helping
visitors browse product categories, view featured items, learn about the
store, and find useful shopping information.

The design uses a dark interface with purple and magenta accents to
create a distinctive technology-focused look.

## Website Sections

-   **Hero section:** Brand introduction, main headline, shopping
    call-to-action, and product visuals.
-   **Product categories:** Quick category links for smartphones, mobile
    accessories, tablets, earbuds, smart watches, power banks, chargers,
    headphones, cases, screen protectors, and more.
-   **Best-selling products:** Product cards with sample product names,
    prices, ratings, and add-to-cart buttons.
-   **Why Smart Telecom:** Store benefits such as fast delivery, secure
    payment, easy returns, and customer support.
-   **About us:** A short introduction to the business and its service
    focus.
-   **Frequently asked questions:** Common questions about product
    authenticity, returns, and delivery charges.
-   **Terms & policies:** A place for store policies and customer
    information.
-   **Call to action and footer:** A final shopping prompt, navigation
    links, and contact/social details.

## Built With

-   **HTML5** --- page structure
-   **CSS3** --- layout, styling, colors, and responsive presentation
-   **JavaScript** --- front-end interactions

## Customize the Project

You are welcome to adapt the project to your needs:

1.  Update the business name, logo, text, and contact details.
2.  Replace the sample product names, images, ratings, and prices with
    your own product information.
3.  Adjust the colors, typography, spacing, and layout in the CSS files.
4.  Review the JavaScript and connect any required functionality to your
    own services.

**Important:** This repository describes a front-end website project.
Product cards, prices, cart controls, contact details, and other sample
content should be checked and connected to a real product database,
payment provider, order system, or backend before using the site for
live commercial transactions. Do not assume that payment processing,
order management, authentication, or inventory synchronization is
included unless you implement and test those features.
